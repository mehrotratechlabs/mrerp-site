Features

Everything from the first bill to the balance sheet.

One program for the counter, the godown and the accountant. Every bill updates stock, the party's ledger and GST together.

01

Purchase, sale and returns

Bills, returns, delivery challans, credit and debit notes and purchase orders. Take payment by cash, credit, UPI or card, or split one bill across them.

02

Stock you can trust

Barcodes, batches and expiry dates, several godowns and transfers between them. Reports for stock movement, non-moving items and expiry.

03

Accounts built in

Every bill posts its own voucher. Ledgers, journal and expense vouchers, cheques and PDC, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet and year closing.

04

GST taken care of

GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-4 and GSTR-9, HSN summary, e-Invoice and e-Way Bill. A GST Check finds mistakes before you file.

05

Supplier bills without typing

Import the bill file your supplier sends from AIOCD or Marg. Any other file can be read by AI, using your own Gemini, Claude or OpenAI key.

06

Every counter, one set of books

One computer is the server; the other counters join over your shop's network and find it on their own. A new item or party shows on every counter straight away.

07

Staff see only their work

Give each user a role for each firm and decide what they can view, add or edit. The audit trail keeps every change made to a bill.

08

Reports that open the bill

Registers, outstanding, rate lists and profit by bill, customer, item or brand. Press Enter on any row to open its bill. Export to Excel, PDF or CSV.

09

Backups you control

Back up to your own disk or your own Google Drive, and restore a single firm or year when you need it. Email a report straight from the screen.

Questions

Before you ask for a demo.

Where is my data kept?

In a database on your own computer or server. We do not receive, copy or store it. The privacy policy says the same in full.

Does it need the internet?

Not for billing, stock, accounts or reports. The internet is used only for what you turn on yourself: emailing reports, backups to your Google Drive and AI bill import.

Can several counters use it together?

Yes. One computer is the server and the other counters connect over your shop's network. They find the server on their own, even after its address changes.

Which businesses is it for?

Pharma, textile, FMCG and electronics traders, wholesale or retail, registered as regular or composition dealers under GST.

Can it read my supplier's bills?

Yes. Purchase import reads the AIOCD invoice file and Marg's bill export formats. A file it cannot read can be passed to AI import, which runs on your own Gemini, Claude or OpenAI key.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
