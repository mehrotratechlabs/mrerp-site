Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

MisterERP ("we") makes inventory and accounting software that you install on your own computers. This policy explains what data we receive and what we do with it.

Your business data stays with you

Your items, parties, bills, vouchers, stock and GST data are stored in a database on your own computer or server. We do not receive, copy or store it.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Google account (Gmail and Google Drive)

You can connect a Google account to MisterERP to email reports and to keep backups in your Google Drive. If you do, MisterERP asks for:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): used only to send the emails you ask MisterERP to send, such as a report to a party. MisterERP cannot read, search or delete your emails.
  • Files created by MisterERP in your Google Drive (drive.file): used only to upload, list, download and delete the backups MisterERP itself creates. MisterERP cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Your email address: to show which account is connected.

The access token Google gives MisterERP is stored, encrypted, in your own MisterERP database. It is never sent to us, and we never see your emails, attachments or backups. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not used to train AI models, is not sold, and is not read by people.

MisterERP's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time in MisterERP, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Your Google data moves only between your own computer and Google.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or raise a grievance, by writing to the address below. We reply within 30 days.

Children

MisterERP is business software and is not meant for children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update it here and change the date at the top.
